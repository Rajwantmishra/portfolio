About

Builder first, strategist by necessity.

I lead a 16–30 person Data Science and Data Engineering organization with direct reports across India, Israel, Milan, and the U.S., and I own AI strategy and delivery for a global enterprise's medical and clinical technology portfolio — 10+ concurrent initiatives at any given time, from automated booking to clinical translation to document processing.

What sets my work apart is that I still build. I designed and built the proprietary NLP/LLM pipeline that replaced our third-party translation vendors, saving roughly $2–3M annually. I architected an internal AI platform (ButlerAI) that abstracts LLM integration, UI, and onboarding behind a single MCP layer, so any team can adopt AI without reinventing the plumbing. I fine-tune models myself when the problem calls for it.

I operate with an entrepreneurial, ideas-to-delivery mindset — identifying where AI creates real value, building the stakeholder buy-in to move on it, and then being the person accountable for shipping it. That combination of technical depth and executive ownership is what I bring to every room.
